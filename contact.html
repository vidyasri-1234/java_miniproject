Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding tracking updates, collection policies, and delivery rescheduling.

How soon after booking does parcel tracking become active?

Electronic waybill records appear in our system immediately upon generation. Checkpoint tracking scans activate as soon as the package is deposited at a depot or collected by our courier driver.

What happens if I am not present during a home delivery attempt?

Our driver leaves a digital missed-delivery calling card. We automatically reattempt delivery on the following business day, or you may elect to collect the item from your nearest regional depot.

How do I file a claim for damaged or missing consignment?

Claims must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the scheduled delivery date. Shippers must provide the original waybill code, photographic evidence of damage, and proof of declared commercial value.

Are import customs duties and taxes included in international shipping?

Standard rates cover transit freight and customs clearance brokerage. Destination customs duties and local value-added taxes (VAT) are assessed by destination country customs authorities unless prepaid via DDP agreement.
