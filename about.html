About Apex Parcel Express

Building trusted, high-performance transport links connecting businesses, retailers, and communities worldwide.

Our Profile

Decades of Ground & Air Logistics Leadership

Founded with the objective of providing uncompromised delivery certainty, Apex Parcel Express has expanded from a regional courier operator into an integrated supply chain partner handling millions of domestic and international parcels each month.

We operate an extensive ground fleet, state-of-the-art sorting hubs, and scheduled air lanes. Every package in our care is backed by transparent tracking, rigorous custody protocols, and dedicated customer support personnel.

Principles

Our Mission, Vision, and Values

The core operating standards that define how we move goods and serve customers every single day.

OUR MISSION

Punctual & Secure Carriage

To deliver every parcel on schedule, intact, and with verifiable accountability, empowering commerce and uniting communities through efficient transport infrastructure.

OUR VISION

Sustainable Network Expansion

To be the most trusted, resource-efficient parcel carrier in the market, setting the standard for on-time precision, digital tracking clarity, and customer responsiveness.

OUR VALUES

We treat every consignment as essential. We prioritize recipient satisfaction, strict handling protocols, transparent pricing with no hidden surcharges, and direct human accountability.

Logistics Infrastructure

Modern Fleet & Sorting Capabilities

Our logistical reach is powered by continuous investments in automated linehaul sorting, telematics, and high-capacity dispatch centers.

Automated Sorting Facilities

Equipped with high-speed optical barcode readers and dynamic weight-dimension scanners capable of handling up to 25,000 packages per hour per depot.

Dedicated Linehaul Network

Point-to-point intercity routes connecting major commercial corridors overnight, eliminating intermediate bottlenecks and minimizing package transfers.

Final-Mile Urban Depots

Strategically positioned micro-hubs in urban centers allowing rapid dispatch, scheduled customer collections, and same-day delivery routing.

Cargo Protection

Strict Chain of Custody & Security

From package deposit to recipient handover, every movement is recorded in our synchronized ledger. We enforce strict identity checks on high-value items, tamper-evident seals for sensitive shipments, and 24/7 CCTV surveillance at all depots.

  • Real-time GPS tracking on all delivery vehicles
  • Standard liability coverage included on all parcels
  • Optional full-value declared cargo insurance up to ₹50,00,000
  • Signature and photographic proof of delivery captured upon receipt
