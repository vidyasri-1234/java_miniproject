Operational Workflow

How Your Parcel Moves Through Our Network

Every shipment follows a strict chain of custody from initial intake to final physical delivery.

Step 01

Booking & Labeling

Generate your standardized waybill label online or deposit your parcel at any certified Apex intake station.

Step 02

Hub Automated Sorting

Packages are scanned, weighed, and sorted at our automated regional distribution centers within minutes.

Step 03

Direct Transit

Dedicated linehaul trucks and air freight lanes transport cargo under continuous temperature and status monitoring.

Step 04

Secure Handover

Local couriers complete final-mile delivery with recipient verification, photo verification, and digital signature.
