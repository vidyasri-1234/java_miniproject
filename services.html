Guidelines

Packaging & Weight Standards

Proper packaging ensures your goods travel securely through automated distribution belts without damage or transit delays.

1. Outer Carton Standards

Always use rigid, corrugated cardboard boxes. Ensure boxes have intact flaps and are not weakened by previous moisture or multiple reuse cycles.

2. Internal Cushioning

Provide at least 5 cm (2 inches) of cushioning material (bubble wrap, air pillows, dense foam) around every item to prevent movement inside the carton.

3. Waybill Label Placement

Attach the printed barcode label flat on the largest surface of the package. Do not place tape or labels over box seams or wrap them around sharp corners.
