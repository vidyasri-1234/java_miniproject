Safety Regulations

Prohibited & Restricted Items

In compliance with international aviation protocols and national transport laws, the following materials cannot be accepted for standard carriage.

Hazardous Materials (HAZMAT)

  • Flammable liquids, gases, and paints
  • Explosives, fireworks, and munitions
  • Corrosive acids, bleaches, and oxidizers
  • Uncertified lithium-ion battery packs

Restricted Goods

  • Perishable fresh produce without refrigeration
  • Live animals or biological specimens
  • Firearms, weapon replicas, and blades
  • Controlled pharmaceuticals without license

High-Value Monetary Items

  • Physical currency, coins, and bullion
  • Unregistered bearer bonds and negotiable notes
  • Loose precious gemstones and unmounted bullion
  • Lottery tickets and gambling tokens

Parcel Collection & Identity Verification Rules

When collecting a package from an Apex Logistics Depot Station or accepting a signature-mandated delivery, recipients must satisfy the following criteria:

  • Government-Issued Photo ID: Valid Passport, Driver’s License, or National Identity Card matching the addressee name.
  • Digital Waybill Notification: Presentation of the official SMS or email dispatch code containing the consignment number.
  • Third-Party Collection: If collecting on behalf of another individual, provide written authorization accompanied by a photocopy of the addressee's official ID.
Parcels held at local depots will remain available for collection for 14 calendar days before return-to-sender procedures commence.
